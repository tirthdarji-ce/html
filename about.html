ABOUT THE PROJECT

About StudentHub

A simple digital campus companion designed around students.

📚 Our Mission

Make education management simple, accessible, and efficient for every student.

🎯 Our Vision

Create a connected learning environment where students can achieve academic success.

💡 Our Values

Accessibility, innovation, collaboration, privacy, and student-focused design.

Project scope

StudentHub brings together registration, login, a dashboard, events, profile details, FAQs, feedback, and administration. The practicals progressively add responsive design, browser interactivity, data storage, and secure PHP/MySQL features.
