Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common StudentHub questions.

How do I create an account?

Open Register, complete the required fields, and submit the form.

How do I sign in?

Use the Login page with the email and password you registered. Database login requires the PHP server.

Where can I find events?

Open Events. Search by event name or filter by category.

Can I update my profile?

The profile page currently demonstrates profile information. Editing is a future backend extension.

How can I contact support?

Use the Contact form to submit your question.

Who can use the Admin page?

In the backend practicals, administrative actions must be protected by role-based access control.
