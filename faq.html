Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about StudentHub, including registration, login, events, profiles, and other student services.

❓ How do I create a StudentHub account?

Go to the Register page, fill in your personal details, and click the Create Account button.

🔑 I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click the Forgot Password link on the Login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

📅 How can I register for college events?

Visit the Events page to view technical and non-technical events. Select the event you wish to participate in and complete the registration process.

👤 Can I update my profile information?

Yes. Open the Profile page where you can view and update your personal and academic details.

📚 Where can I find study materials?

Study materials, assignments, and academic resources are available through your Student Dashboard.

📞 How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page to find our email address, phone number, and office location.

Need More Help?

If your question is not listed above, please visit the Contact page and reach out to our support team. We are always happy to assist you.
